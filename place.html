Outdoors · Entoto

Entoto Park

Entoto Park is a recreational destination in the Entoto area, known for its natural surroundings and views of Addis Ababa. It offers visitors a chance to enjoy outdoor spaces away from the busiest parts of the city.

What makes it interesting?

  • Green surroundings and outdoor spaces.
  • Views across parts of Addis Ababa.
  • An opportunity to enjoy nature and outdoor activities.

Visitor reminder: Check current access information, opening hours and any fees before planning a visit.

ቅርስHeritage
Heritage · Museum

National Museum of Ethiopia

The National Museum of Ethiopia is an important place to learn about the country's archaeological, historical and artistic heritage. Its collections help visitors understand different periods of Ethiopia's past.

Why visit?

  • Explore exhibitions related to Ethiopian history and heritage.
  • Learn about archaeology and cultural history.
  • Discover objects and stories connected to the country's past.

Visitor reminder: Confirm current opening hours, ticket information and photography rules with the museum before visiting.

Heritage · Gardens

Unity Park

Unity Park is a visitor destination within the grounds of the National Palace. It presents aspects of Ethiopia's history, heritage, nature and cultural diversity.

What can visitors explore?

  • Heritage and historical exhibits.
  • Gardens and landscaped outdoor areas.
  • Displays connected to Ethiopia's cultural diversity.

Visitor reminder: Check current booking requirements, opening hours and visitor rules before your trip.

ገበያMarket
Market · Everyday life

Merkato

Merkato is widely known as one of Addis Ababa's major open-air market areas. It is a busy commercial district where visitors can experience the energy of everyday trade and find a wide variety of goods.

What makes Merkato distinctive?

  • A lively atmosphere shaped by local businesses and shoppers.
  • A wide range of goods and market activities.
  • A glimpse into the city's everyday commercial life.

Visitor reminder: Keep personal belongings secure and ask permission before photographing people or businesses.

Before you go

Plan your visit responsibly.

Opening hours, prices, access rules and transport arrangements can change. Confirm details through official or trusted sources before travelling.
