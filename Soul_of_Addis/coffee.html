Origins

Ethiopia and coffee

Ethiopia is strongly associated with coffee and is known for a rich coffee culture. Coffee is enjoyed in homes, cafés, workplaces and social gatherings. Its importance goes beyond the drink itself: preparing and sharing coffee can create time for people to connect.

The traditional coffee ceremony

Five stages of welcome

The Ethiopian coffee ceremony is a social tradition in which coffee is prepared and served to guests. It is often performed carefully and can be an important expression of welcome and respect.

  1. Preparing

    Green coffee beans are cleaned and prepared for roasting.

  2. Roasting

    The beans are roasted, traditionally in a pan, until they develop their aroma.

  3. Grinding

    The roasted beans are ground before brewing.

  4. Brewing

    The coffee is prepared in a traditional pot called a jebena.

  5. Serving

    Coffee is poured into small cups and shared with guests.

Together

Coffee and community

Coffee is the reason to stay a little longer.

A coffee ceremony can bring family members, neighbors and friends together. It creates a setting for conversation, storytelling, welcoming guests and spending time with one another.

The ceremony may take place during ordinary visits as well as special gatherings. The details can vary between families and communities. Guests are often served more than one round of coffee, so it is worth settling in.

Origins

Coffee-growing regions

Ethiopia has several coffee-growing areas, and different growing conditions contribute to a wide range of coffee characteristics.

i.

Yirgacheffe

Known internationally for distinctive specialty coffees.

ii.

Sidama

A major coffee-producing area with varied growing environments.

iii.

Harrar

A historic coffee-growing area in eastern Ethiopia.

iv.

Jimma

An important coffee-producing area in southwestern Ethiopia.

Coffee characteristics vary by origin, variety, processing method and preparation. A region name alone does not guarantee a particular flavor.

Good manners

Experiencing a ceremony

If you are invited to a coffee ceremony, take time to enjoy the experience. Be respectful of the host's customs, ask questions politely, and remember that the gathering is about hospitality as much as coffee.
