Celebrations through the year ↗
በዓል

Cultural celebrations

Addis Ababa hosts celebrations connected to Ethiopia's diverse religious, cultural and national traditions, with gatherings, music, food, ceremonies and public activities.

ጥበብ

Art and music events

Art exhibitions, live music, theatre, film screenings and creative gatherings offer chances to discover local artists and different forms of expression.

ማህበረሰብ

Community activities

Public discussions, educational events, markets, workshops, sports activities and neighborhood gatherings connect residents and visitors with the city.

Through the year

Widely observed celebrations

Examples of widely observed celebrations in Ethiopia include Enkutatash, Meskel, Timkat and Eid. The way each occasion is observed can vary among communities.

  • 11Sep

    Enkutatashእንቁጣጣሽ

    The Ethiopian New Year, welcomed at the end of the rainy season, with family gatherings, flowers and festive meals.

  • 27Sep

    Meskelመስቀል

    A public celebration associated with the finding of the True Cross, marked with gatherings, ceremonies and a large bonfire (demera).

  • 07Jan

    Gennaገና

    Ethiopian Christmas, observed with church services and shared meals with family and friends.

  • 19Jan

    Timkatጥምቀት

    Celebrates Epiphany with colourful processions, music and people dressed in traditional white clothing.

  • 02Mar

    Adwa Victory Dayየዓድዋ ድል

    A national day remembering the Battle of Adwa, with commemorations, public events and cultural programmes.

  • —Moon-based

    Eid celebrationsኢድ

    Observed by Muslim communities. Dates follow the Islamic lunar calendar, so they move each year.

Dates shown are typical Gregorian dates and can shift by a day from year to year. Always confirm with local announcements.

Before you attend

Event information status: This student project page introduces event categories and annual celebrations. It does not list confirmed upcoming events.

  1. Confirm the date, time and venue with the organizer.
  2. Check whether registration or tickets are required.
  3. Look for transport, accessibility and entry information.
  4. Follow the venue's rules and respect other attendees.
